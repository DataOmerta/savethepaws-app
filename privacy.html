Privacy Policy for SaveThePaws

Last Updated: October 2024

SaveThePaws is an independent civic technology project. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we handle your data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

To facilitate animal rescue, we collect only the data necessary for the app to function:

  • User-Submitted Content: Photos, brief descriptions, and animal condition tags you voluntarily submit when reporting a stray, lost, or injured animal.
  • Location Data (Optional): GPS coordinates are captured only when you explicitly grant location permissions. This is used solely to pinpoint the animal's location for rescue coordination.
  • Account Information (For Subscribers): If you choose to upgrade to a premium subscriber tier, we collect basic account identifiers to manage your subscription and enable features like viewing uploader identities and claiming animals.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To connect you with local rescuers, NGOs, and potential adopters.
  • To run automated AI image analysis and keyword filtering to verify reports and prevent spam.
  • To display reports on the live rescue coordination map for verified subscribers.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your data. We do not use third-party analytics, and we do not serve advertisements.

Location data and report details are visible only to other users of the SaveThePaws application (specifically, verified subscribers for precise coordinates) for the sole purpose of facilitating animal rescue. We do not share your personal data with external commercial entities or data brokers.

4. Data Storage and Security

Free users' basic data is kept to a minimum. All data transmission is secured using industry-standard encryption. We retain user-submitted reports only as long as necessary to facilitate the rescue or adoption of the animal, after which they can be deleted upon request.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Deny location permissions (you can still manually type a general location or use the app without GPS).

6. Children's Privacy

SaveThePaws is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
