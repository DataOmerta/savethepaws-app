Account Deletion Request for SaveThePaws

Last Updated: October 2024

At SaveThePaws, we respect your right to control your personal data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please follow the simple steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

Step 1: Send an email to [INSERT YOUR CONTACT EMAIL HERE]

Step 2: Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request - SaveThePaws"

Step 3: In the body of the email, include the email address or username associated with the SaveThePaws account you wish to delete.

For security and verification purposes, we may reply to confirm that the request is legitimate before processing the deletion.

What Data is Deleted

Upon processing your request, we will permanently delete the following data associated with your account:

  • Your account credentials (username, email address, and password hash).
  • All user-generated content you have uploaded, including photos, descriptions, and animal condition reports.
  • Your precise location history and GPS coordinates saved within the app.
  • Your subscriber status and any associated premium access privileges.

Data Retention Period

All personal data and user-generated content associated with your account will be permanently and irreversibly deleted from our active servers and backups within 30 days of receiving and verifying your valid deletion request.

Note: Anonymized, aggregate statistical data (e.g., "50 animals rescued in this city this month") that can no longer be linked to you personally may be retained for civic reporting purposes.
